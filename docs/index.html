ORPHAN FEED — RAW

Ungrouped orphan incidents: candidate new taxonomy categories. Threshold: 20/quarter.
METHODOLOGY. Two signal layers, never summed: confirmed (hard triggers — regulator action, litigation filed, company acknowledgment, credible NGO campaign) and indicative (media salience composite). Company–article links enter the incident layer only when the company's Event Registry concept salience score is ≥ 4 or the company is named in the article title — passing mentions (bystanders) stay in the raw recall layer (2172 low-salience links currently held back) and form no incidents. Salience z-scores are computed per company against its own 90-day median/MAD baseline of controversy-relevant coverage and stay inactive (THIN) until 30 days of history accumulate — silence before that is never treated as clean. State changes (WATCH → FLAGGED → EXCLUDED) are human decisions recorded with full evidence chains; this dashboard is read-only. Channel B clusters orphan incidents into emergent themes by shared concept signatures (top-8 concepts, generic concepts above 8% document frequency excluded, ≥3 shared to merge); escalation scores are uncalibrated priors until the replay harness yields labelled outcomes. Company impact is scored on three separate axes that are NEVER summed: SEV (issue substance, size-adjusted via the company's own baseline — raw volume is never an input), OUTRAGE (raw visibility × moral charge of the issue — the sustainable-fund headline risk), and MAT (expected share-price response in CAR bps; shown as UNCAL until the FMP event-study calibration produces an evidence table — never zero-filled). Parameter register v0.4.0 (config/parameters.yaml); thresholds: z_watch 2.0 / z_flag 3.0 / z_exclude 4.0. Sweep: newsapi.ai batched concept queries, 40-keyword server-side recall filter.
